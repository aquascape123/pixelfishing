Privacy Policy for Pixel Fishing

App Name: Pixel Fishing
Package Name: com.aquascape.pixelfishing
Developer: AquaScape (GitHub: aquascape123)
Effective Date: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy governs your use of the mobile application Pixel Fishing, developed and published by AquaScape. We take the privacy of our players seriously and are committed to maintaining a safe, transparent gaming environment.

1. Information Collection and Storage

Pixel Fishing does NOT collect, store, transmit, or sell any Personally Identifiable Information (PII), including but not limited to real names, email addresses, phone numbers, contact lists, physical addresses, or precise GPS location data.

A. Local Storage & Gameplay Progress

  • All gameplay data (such as collected coins, fishing rod and reel upgrade levels, fish catch records, and unlocked encyclopedia species) is stored locally on your device using local application storage (PlayerPrefs).
  • No game progress is transmitted or synced to any remote or external server.

B. Device Permissions

  • Vibration / Haptic Feedback (android.permission.VIBRATE): Utilized exclusively to provide tactile sensations during gameplay (such as when a fish strikes or during line tension warnings). No vibration data is stored or shared.

2. Third-Party Services & Game Engine

Pixel Fishing is built using the Unity engine. Unity Technologies may collect non-identifiable technical diagnostic data strictly required to render graphics and run the game engine (e.g., operating system version, hardware specifications, screen resolution, and crash logs).

For more details regarding Unity's data practices, please consult the Unity Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising and Analytics

Pixel Fishing currently contains no third-party advertising networks and no behavioral tracking analytics. If advertising networks (such as Google AdMob) or Google Play Games Services are introduced in future versions, this document will be updated in full compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and applicable data privacy regulations.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

Pixel Fishing is family-friendly and designed for a general audience. Because our game does not collect personal data from any user, it inherently protects children's privacy and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all save data is stored locally on your device:

  • You can reset your save data at any time through your device's Settings > Apps > Pixel Fishing > Clear Storage.
  • Uninstalling the application permanently removes all saved files, records, and settings from your device.

6. Policy Updates

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be posted directly to this URL with a revised effective date.
