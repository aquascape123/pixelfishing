This Privacy Policy governs your use of the mobile game Pixel Fishing, developed and published by AquaDev Creations ("we", "us", or "our"). We are dedicated to ensuring a transparent, secure, and privacy-respecting experience for all players.

1. Information Collection and Storage

Pixel Fishing does NOT collect, store, transmit, or sell any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) (such as real names, email addresses, phone numbers, contact lists, physical addresses, or precise location data).

A. Local Gameplay Data & Storage

  • All gameplay progress (collected coins, rod and reel upgrade levels, fish catch records, and unlocked encyclopedia entries) is stored locally on your device using local application storage (PlayerPrefs).
  • No game progress or telemetry is sent or synchronized to any external or cloud servers.

B. Device Permissions

  • Vibration / Haptic Feedback (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used solely to provide tactile sensations during fishing gameplay (such as fish bites and line tension warnings). No vibration data is recorded or transmitted.

2. Third-Party Services & Game Engine

Pixel Fishing is built using the Unity game engine. Unity Technologies may collect non-identifiable technical diagnostic data strictly required to run the game engine (e.g., operating system version, hardware specifications, screen resolution, and crash logs).

For more details regarding Unity's data practices, please review the Unity Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising and Analytics

Pixel Fishing currently contains no third-party advertising networks and no user tracking analytics. If advertising networks (such as Google AdMob) are integrated in future updates, this policy will be promptly updated in full compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

Pixel Fishing is family-friendly and designed for a general audience. Because our game does not collect personal data from any user, it inherently protects children's privacy and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion (User Data Rights)

Because all save data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your data at all times:

  • Manual Reset: You can reset all in-game progress at any time via your device Settings: Settings > Apps > Pixel Fishing > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data.
  • App Uninstallation: Uninstalling the application permanently deletes all saved game progress, records, and settings from your device.
  • Formal Data Deletion Request: If you have questions regarding your data or wish to submit a formal data deletion or privacy request, please contact us at:
Email: aquadev.creations@gmail.com
Subject: Data Deletion Request - Pixel Fishing
We will review and respond to your request promptly within 48 hours.
